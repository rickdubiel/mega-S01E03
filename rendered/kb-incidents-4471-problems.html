parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. These reports come in by app, email and booking-v2 forms. This is the bug #4471 names, and its queued fix covers this row only.

hint: booking-v3 prints a month-first hint

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint tells them, and the form flips it. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3 land here. The parser fix does not reach them: the hint, or how the form reads it, has to change.

one-day: dates move by one day

These reports do not describe a flipped day and month. The date moves by one day: an invite one day off, one meeting on Tuesday in Lisbon and on Monday in Toronto, an invite that arrived before the booking was made. A day-first parser cannot do that; time-zone handling can. The desk listed them in desk-notes.json and footnoted them under parser. They are a bug of their own. The counts follow the desk: these reports stay in the parser count, where they are filed, and are listed again here.

Intake tags are not causes. Intake guessed hint from a US browser locale; the cause depends on the tenant and its form. The "tagged as" column shows where the two disagree.
